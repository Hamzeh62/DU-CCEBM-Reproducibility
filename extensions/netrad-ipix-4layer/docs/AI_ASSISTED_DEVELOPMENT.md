# Research software provenance and AI assistance

The author **Hamzeh Ghahramani** developed the classical CCEBM code and the DU-CCEBM extension with assistance from generative AI tools. Claude assisted with `ccebm_v3.py`, `pu_lattice.py`, and the helper module `cerbm.py`; ChatGPT assisted with the DU implementation, tests, documentation and reproducibility materials. The author confirms rights to distribute his original software, performed and verified the experimental research, and is responsible for the code, data processing, figures and claims.

This is a code-provenance disclosure, not an authorship or co-inventorship attribution to AI tools. AI participation should also be disclosed in the manuscript's Methods (code development) and, where applicable, in the Elsevier manuscript-preparation declaration. The source and generated results remain subject to human scientific validation.
